# Lamborghini Murciélago SV V12 engine pack

`cut.mp3` is cut from the audio of **"Lamborghini Murcielago SV / Pure Sound
/ Loudest Sound" by Best Supercars Sound** on YouTube,
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3xZiKHKOs7A.

The video: a Murciélago LP670-4 SuperVeloce (6.5 L V12) idling and revving
while stationary, filmed from outside the car.

**Licence:** the video is published under YouTube's Creative Commons
Attribution licence (reuse allowed), as shown on its watch page. Attribution
is required: this file is it, and `pack.json` names the channel as well.

Changes made here: the first 11.4 s of `cut.mp3` are 92.0 to 103.4 s of the
video, the engine idling at about 915 rpm, with the loop point crossfaded;
then 0.2 to 6.6 s, a slow rev from 1400 to 5000 rpm; then 21.6 to 24.5 s, a
quick rev that reaches the 8000 rpm redline. The keyframes chain the slow rev
for the lower half of the range and the quick one for the upper half. The
rpm values in `pack.json` were estimated from the firing-frequency harmonics
(six firings per revolution), anchored on the idle and cross-checked against
the strongest spectral peaks, not read from the car's tachometer.
