# Lexus LFA V10 engine pack

`cut.mp3` is cut from the audio of **"BLUE Lexus LFA Start Up, Loud Revs,
Acceleration" by Best Sport CARS Video** on YouTube,
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WriEjtaC-Hc.

The video: a blue Lexus LFA starting up, idling, revving while stationary,
and pulling away, filmed from outside the car. The 4.8 L 1LR-GUE V10 revs to
9000 rpm.

**Licence:** the video is published under YouTube's Creative Commons
Attribution licence (reuse allowed), as shown on its watch page. Attribution
is required: this file is it, and `pack.json` names the channel as well.

Changes made here: the first 5.2 s of `cut.mp3` are 50.6 to 55.8 s of the
video, the engine at its fast idle of about 1100 rpm after a cold start, with
the loop point crossfaded; the rest is 86.2 to 89.4 s, the car pulling away
in first gear from 1350 to about 6800 rpm. Everything above that is
pitch-shifted up to the 9000 rpm redline. The rpm values in `pack.json` were
estimated from the firing-frequency harmonics (five firings per revolution),
anchored on the idle and cross-checked against the strongest spectral peaks,
not read from the car's tachometer. The LFA revs so fast that its stationary
revs give almost no usable material in the middle of the range, which is why
the pull-away was used instead.
