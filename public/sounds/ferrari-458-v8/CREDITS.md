# Ferrari 458 Italia V8 engine pack

`cut.mp3` is cut from the audio of **"Ferrari 458 Italia exhaust sound by
Akrapovic" by Motor1** on YouTube, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SDekeCbqWJI.

The video: a Ferrari 458 Italia fitted with an Akrapovic exhaust, idling and
revving while stationary, recorded for the Akrapovic product film. The 4.5 L
flat-plane V8 is stock; the exhaust is not.

**Licence:** the video is published under YouTube's Creative Commons
Attribution licence (reuse allowed), as shown on its watch page. Attribution
is required: this file is it, and `pack.json` names the channel as well.

Changes made here: the first 11 s of `cut.mp3` are 82.5 to 93.5 s of the
video, the engine idling at about 930 rpm, with the loop point crossfaded;
the rest is 60.6 to 65.3 s, one rev from idle to the 9000 rpm limiter with a
short pause at 3500 rpm on the way up. The rpm values in `pack.json` were
estimated from the firing-frequency harmonics (four firings per revolution),
anchored on the idle, not read from the car's tachometer.
